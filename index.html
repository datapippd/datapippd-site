How it works

From raw inputs to governed outputs

Inputs are shaped into the governed layer. Everything that reads it, from APIs and events to BI and AI, can change without touching the truth.

Ingest

Databases, APIs, IoT, third-party systems via batch, streaming, and CDC.

Taxonomy

Map to a unified taxonomy that stays governed across clouds.

Masters + registry

UPM, UCM, UIM plus Attribute Registry extensions for business needs.

Exposure

REST and GraphQL, Kafka and webhooks, BI and AI endpoints. Disposable consumers of the layer.

The durable asset

Governed masters, taxonomy, Attribute Registry

This is the layer we install. Each client gets its own, on its own platform: Snowflake, Databricks, Azure or AWS. Screens, APIs, apps and AI agents sit on top. We structure first, because analytics, automation and AI all assume the data is already right.

Master Data Layer

Strictly governed entities

  • Universal Product Master (UPM)
  • Universal Client Master (UCM)
  • Universal Identity Master (UIM)

Application Extensions

Dynamic attributes via Attribute Registry

  • Business-specific fields without breaking the core
  • Versioned hierarchy control
  • Ready for RPA, ERP, CRM, BI and AI agents

AI on evidence

Every data and process service we run exists for one purpose: AI that works on facts you can trace.

Each statement carries the source it came from, a separate check, and a record of who cleared it. Nothing moves until it clears.

Discover

Interviews, transcripts and documents in. Processes, roles, data, rules and pain points out, each with its source, plus an agenda of what is still unproven.

Diagnose

The as-is of an operating model, read from your own systems and documents, with evidence, in a fixed week. For companies that want AI and have no governance yet.

Govern

Glossary, ownership, classification and rule registers for your data catalogue. Every entry carries the clause it came from. Conflicts stay open until an owner decides.

Clear

The gate on a migration or a transformation. Every object is checked for classification, ownership, rules and disposition before it moves, with proof of why.

Feed

Verified statements land as governed tables in your own platform, with provenance, versioned and deletable by subject.

Audit trail

What wrote it, what checked it, which source it quotes and which version of the rules applied. Every transformation leaves a record an auditor can follow.
