About Datapippd

An independent data practice.

Datapippd is an independent data practice, based in Warsaw and working across Europe since 2024. We build the governed data layer of an organisation, the technology that serves it and the operating model that keeps it true. Then we measure what the business gets.

Our work has run inside a European market infrastructure, global banks and custodians, and retail and pharmacy businesses. When an engagement needs more than one practice, we deliver with a small network of senior partners in operations and engineering.

We do not sell slides or headcount. Every engagement starts with a fixed Review and grows only on evidence.
